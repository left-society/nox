Focus indicator — 10 layouts

Each card shows a different way to surface "Focus is on, nox is being quiet." Toggle below to compare with Focus off (some variants are no-ops in that state). Pill is rendered at ~1.27× true size for legibility — real resting pill is 220×20pt.

Trailing moon (current)

01

Moon sits after the waveform at the right edge. Subtle, doesn't fight any element. Hardest to notice — that's the whole design.

+ minimal, easy to miss in a good way – may be TOO subtle; fights waveform attention

Leading moon

02

Moon at the left edge, before the artwork. Reads first since you scan left-to-right.

+ first thing the eye sees – pushes the artwork off its anchor; less calm

Replace waveform

03

When Focus is on, the bouncy waveform is replaced by a static moon. Honest about the "calm down" intent.

+ visually quieter pill, matches Focus mood – loses "audio is flowing" signal during Focus

In real impl, the waveform would actually hide; this mockup shows both for layout clarity.

Corner badge (top-right)

04

Tiny lavender moon as a badge floating off the top-right corner. iOS-app-icon vibe.

+ doesn't compete with pill content at all – the pill's outline is the silhouette of the notch — protruding badges break that

Artwork badge

05

Tiny moon overlay on the bottom-right corner of the album art. Like an iMessage typing-indicator pip on a contact photo.

+ pill silhouette stays clean; left-side anchor – 9pt at retina is on the edge of legibility

Lavender pill tint

06

No glyph. The pill itself takes a deep lavender tint while Focus is on. The whole thing IS the indicator.

+ ambient, not visual noise; brand-aligned – no clear "moon"=Focus mapping, easy to miss

Border halo

07

Soft lavender outer glow around the pill while Focus is on. Pill stays black; the halo is the cue.

+ premium, ambient; matches other Apple "system mode" cues – costs more to render (blur radius); could leak past the notch silhouette

"Focus" pill (text + moon)

08

Pill grows by ~40pt to fit "Focus" text after the waveform, with a thin divider. Most explicit option.

Focus

+ unambiguous; readable at a glance – pill width fluctuates with state, which fights the locked 220pt resting geometry

Breathing moon

09

Trailing moon (like 01) but with a slow 3.2s breath. The motion is the "this is alive, intentional" signal.

+ noticeable without being loud; says "I'm intentionally on" – breathing animations can feel "demanding attention" — opposite of Focus

Top notch accent

10

A thin lavender line ABOVE the pill (where the menu bar / hardware notch is) plus a tiny moon at the trailing edge of the pill itself. Two-zone signal.

+ uses the dead zone above the pill; brand-color anchor – the menu bar / hardware notch sits there in real life — the line could collide visually
