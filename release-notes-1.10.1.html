nox 1.10.1

Sharper now-playing sync, clearer voice capture, and a round of fixes.

Improved

  • Album art framing is now on by default — your music gets the animated decoration out of the box. Toggle it in Settings → Now Playing.
  • The music waveform now flows through a soft gradient instead of a flat color.
  • Voice capture controls. The capture bar now has a clear Cancel and Done, plus a live “Transcribing…” indicator so you know your recording is being processed.
  • A refreshed welcome and feature tour for new installs.
  • Lighter and smoother — less memory use and steadier scrolling, especially on 8 GB Macs.

Fixed

  • Now Playing reflects the true state. No more showing a track as playing when it’s actually paused, and the Spotify timeline stays in step.
  • Tasks honor the day you type. Saying “tomorrow” (or “tonight”) now schedules for the right day instead of today.
  • Teleprompter: the close button no longer gets clipped at the rounded corner.
  • Sharing: fixed an occasional failure when sharing a note or file.
