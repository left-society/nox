nox 1.9.5

The first-launch experience reworked from scratch.

  • Onboarding now leads, permissions follow. Every macOS permission has its own onboarding step with a privacy explainer in plain English. Microphone, Accessibility, Calendar, and Music — each one tells you exactly what it unlocks, what nox does not do, and what happens if you skip. The system prompt only fires after you tap Allow.
  • Auto-relocate to /Applications. If you launch nox from the DMG or from Downloads, it offers to move itself to /Applications and relaunch. Auto-update and saved permissions actually work after that.
  • No more permission dialogs over a blank window. The Accessibility prompt was firing at launch before onboarding had painted. Held back until the user reaches the Accessibility step.
  • Music card permission deferred. Spotify/Apple Music Apple Events prompts now wait until after onboarding, with a step explaining what they're for.

Your data stays on your Mac. nox never transmits or stores audio, calendar events, or media metadata.
