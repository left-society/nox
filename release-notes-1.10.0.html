nox 1.10.0

Album-art decorations, a livelier waveform, and a round of polish.

New

  • Album art decorations. Frame your music with an animated ring that shifts with every track. Turn it on in Settings → Now Playing.

Improved

  • The music waveform moves more naturally and now responds to your volume.
  • Teleprompter: the Start button stays reachable on long scripts, and the pause and close controls are always visible while you read.
  • Tasks & Calendar: times you type are now honored, finishing a dated task clears it from Calendar, and a new expandable month view lets you browse your schedule inline.

Fixed

  • Smoothed out an occasional flicker in the volume indicator.
  • The notch no longer sometimes ignores your cursor.
