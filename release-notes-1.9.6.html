nox 1.9.6

Setup is now painless for everyone, not just power users.

  • Accessibility is no longer required to start dictating. Default hotkey is now ⌘⇧D, which uses a Carbon hotkey that needs zero permissions. Hold-Fn-to-talk is still there, just opt-in via Settings → Dictation when you want it.
  • Clipboard fallback for paste. If you skip Accessibility, your transcribed text lands on the clipboard with a one-tap "Paste" hint instead of silently going nowhere. ⌘V to paste.
  • Onboarding trimmed from 6 steps to 5. Accessibility step dropped from the default flow. Microphone is the only "must allow" prompt. Calendar and Music are still optional.
  • The Settings → Dictation pane now offers "Press ⌘⇧D" as the new default mode.

Your data stays on your Mac. nox never transmits or stores audio, calendar events, or media metadata.
