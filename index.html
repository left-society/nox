A clear promise

Your stuff stays on your Mac.

When you talk, your voice becomes text right on your Mac. Nothing goes online. nox doesn't track what you do, doesn't ask you to make an account, and doesn't have a server we send your stuff to. The only thing nox ever sends online is a quick check for new updates. That's it.

What nox keeps on your Mac

  • Notes~/Library/Application Support/nox/notes.db
  • Screenshots you drop in~/Library/Application Support/nox/Images/
  • Videos you saved~/Library/Application Support/nox/Videos/
  • Files you parked~/Library/Application Support/nox/Files/
  • Settings and hotkeysapp.trynox plist
  • Cached album artin memory only, never written

Drag nox.app to Trash. Everything above goes with it.

What nox never collects

  • Account info. There is no account.
  • Your name, email, or phone.
  • Telemetry, analytics, crash reports.
  • What you typed, said, or pasted.
  • Which apps you use, when, or for how long.
  • Anything from your other tabs or windows.

The only thing nox ever asks the internet for is "any updates?" once every few hours.

Common questions

Everything you might wonder.

Quick answers. Email us if anything is missing.

Does it work on Macs without a notch?

Yes. On non-notched MacBooks and external displays the panel sits at the top center of the screen and behaves the same way. The notch is a nice landmark, not a requirement.

How do I open the panel?

Move your cursor to the dark cutout. The panel grows out. Move your cursor away to close it. You can also press ⌥ Space from anywhere.

Which music apps does it work with?

Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube in any browser, podcasts, audiobooks, anything that publishes Now Playing info to macOS. Skip and pause work for Spotify and Apple Music. For everything else you still see what's playing.

Will it slow my Mac down?

No. Tiny native binary. Zero CPU when nothing is happening. No background polling, no telemetry. Built to feel like part of the Mac, not a window stuck on top of it.

Does it support multiple monitors?

Yes. nox follows whichever screen has your focused window. Plug in a second display and it shows up on whichever notch you're working with.

What permissions does it ask for?

All optional. Calendar to show today's events. Microphone for voice to text. Accessibility so the hotkey works system wide. Deny any one and the rest still works.

How do I uninstall it?

Drag nox.app to Trash. Everything nox kept on your Mac (notes, screenshots, videos, settings) sits inside the app's container, so it goes with it. Nothing left behind.

What does it cost?

Free while we're testing. One time price at v1.0. We will email beta users the number first. No subscription, no in app upgrades, no "pro" tier. Buy it once, own it forever.

Can I get my notes out?

Yes. Notes export as plain Markdown. Screenshots and videos are already plain files on disk. You always own your stuff and can take it anywhere.
