Questions? Answers.

Do I need a Mac with a notch?

No. A notch makes it feel native, but nox works on any modern Mac. It draws its own pill region at the top-center of the screen where a notch would be.

Is my data private?

By design. Voice transcription and the AI clean-up run entirely on your Mac (Whisper on the Apple Neural Engine), and your audio file is deleted the moment it's transcribed. Your files, screenshots and notes are stored locally. The only things that ever touch the network are optional features you turn on with your own API key, and checking for app updates.

Will it slow my Mac down?

No. nox is native Swift and sits at essentially 0% CPU when you're not using it. It only wakes when you reach for it, and it suspends its background helpers when nothing's playing.

Which Macs are supported?

macOS 15 or later. Apple Silicon is recommended, since that's what runs the on-device Whisper and AI features fastest. The download is about 16 MB.

What can play in the music pill?

Spotify, Apple Music, Apple Podcasts, and anything playing in a browser. That covers YouTube, SoundCloud, Twitch and Vimeo, across Chrome, Safari, Arc, Brave, Edge and others. It keeps working on recent macOS versions where some notch apps stopped detecting media.

Is it really free?

Yes. While we're testing, every feature is unlocked, with no account and no credit card. Early testers will be looked after when we settle on pricing later.
