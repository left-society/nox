What it does

One small surface. Everything you needed open windows for.

Hover the notch, get the slab. Move your cursor away, it's gone. No focus stolen, no Mission Control hop.

♪

Music card

Now-playing artwork with two-faced flip. Spotify, Apple Music, browser audio — all in one transport.

⌥

Voice, on your Mac

Hold Fn and speak. Whisper transcribes locally. Audio never leaves the device — no servers, no recordings stored.

⤓

Drag & drop

Drag any file to the notch to stage it. Images, videos, arbitrary files — all retrievable from the slab later.

▶

Browser video grab

Press ⌥⌘V on any browser tab. nox pulls the video down with yt-dlp and tucks it into the videos shelf.

▦

Calendar pill

Your next meeting on the notch with a one-tap join. Read-only — events never leave Apple's Calendar app.

⚡

System events

Charging, AirDrop arrivals, Bluetooth, screenshots, timers. Everything that interrupts you, beautifully.

Privacy posture

Your data stays on your Mac.

Voice transcription runs on-device through Whisper. API keys live in your keychain, never in plaintext. No analytics, no usage tracking, no account required. The app updates itself by checking one signed feed — that's the entire network surface.
