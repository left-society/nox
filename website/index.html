Questions everyone asks.

Does it work on Macs without a notch?

Yes. On non-notched MacBooks and external displays, the halo sits at top-center fused with the menu bar. Same gestures, same behavior. The notch hardware is a nice landmark, not a requirement.

How is this different from Alcove?

Alcove is a music HUD. nox is the same idea, plus a notes app, plus an image inbox, plus a video downloader, plus a file shelf, plus AI summarisation, plus an AirDrop pill, plus a calendar pill. If you only want music, Alcove is great. If you want one quiet place for everything, that's us.

Will it slow my Mac down?

No. Pure Swift, native AppKit, tiny binary. Zero CPU when idle — no background polling above 4 Hz, no telemetry calls, no Electron runtime.

Where does my data go?

Your Mac. That's it. Notes, screenshots, videos, files all live in ~/Library/Application Support/nox. No cloud, no account, no analytics.

What does it cost?
