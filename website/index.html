Reclaim your notch.

Notetaker is a small Mac app that quietly lives around your MacBook's notch. Notes, screenshots, music, and video downloads — without leaving what you're doing.

Download for Mac how it works ↓

Free during beta. macOS 13 or later.

The notch opens when you reach for it.

Drift your cursor toward the camera and the panel teases open. Stay there a fraction of a second longer and it blooms into a full slab. Move away and it's gone again. No hotkey to remember, no menubar icon to hunt for.

Anything you copy from a browser, a PDF, a chat — it's a row in Notes the moment you let go of ⌘C.
From an editor, terminal, or password manager, it isn't.

Shift-Cmd-4 has a home now.

Every screenshot lands in Notetaker. Take just one and it disappears in a few hours. Take two within three seconds and Notetaker decides you meant it, and keeps them.

Two hotkeys.
Everything else routes itself.

Open the panel
⌥Space
Grab the front tab as a video
⌥⌘V
Take a screenshot (system)
⇧⌘4
Copy a note back to clipboard
⌘C
Dismiss
esc

No cloud. No accounts. No telemetry.
Your notes live in ~/Library/Application Support/Notetaker.

Notetaker doesn't ship a backend. There is no analytics, no sign‑in, no «allow notifications» popup. Uninstalling is dragging a single .app to the Trash.
