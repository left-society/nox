Reclaim your notch.

Notetaker is a small Mac app that quietly lives around your MacBook's notch. Notes, screenshots, music, and video downloads — without leaving what you're doing.

Download for Mac how it works ↓

Free during beta. macOS 13 or later.

The notch opens
when you reach for it.

Drift your cursor toward the camera and the panel teases open. Stay there a fraction of a second longer and it blooms into a full slab. Move away and it's gone again. No hotkey to remember, no menubar icon to hunt for.

And the rest of it.

Now Playing
The pill shows whatever's coming out of macOS — Spotify, Music, Safari, any source the system knows about. Hover for transport.
AI summary
Drop your own Gemini key in Settings and any note collapses into a tight summary on demand. Your key, your model, your data.
Drag & drop
Start dragging an image and the panel pops to Images. A video file lands in Videos. The panel meets you halfway, on whatever surface you're on.
Charging pill
Plug in and a small pill blooms briefly to confirm. Same on unplug. It never asks for attention; it just lets you know.
Self-cleaning
Anything you don't engage with rolls into trash after two days, deletes after a week. Tunable in Settings if you're a hoarder.
Skips your editor
⌘C inside Xcode, VS Code, Terminal, iTerm, Notion, Obsidian, your password manager — Notetaker recognises the context and stays out of the way.
Burst detection
Take one screenshot and it expires in a few hours. Take two within three seconds and Notetaker decides you meant it — both stay.
Quiet by default
No Dock icon. No badge. No notification permission popup on first launch. The menubar only carries the icon if you ask it to.
Launch at login
One toggle in Settings. Uses the modern SMAppService API, not a hidden launch agent.
Native and light
Pure Swift and SwiftUI. The whole bundle is 4.5 MB. Launches instantly. Sits in roughly 12 MB of RAM. Behaves like a Mac app because it is one.

No cloud. No accounts. No telemetry.
Your notes live in ~/Library/Application Support/Notetaker.

Notetaker doesn't ship a backend. There is no analytics, no sign‑in, no «allow notifications» popup. Uninstalling is dragging a single .app to the Trash.
